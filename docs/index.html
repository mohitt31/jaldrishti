Not a household safety tool: a survey value says nothing about your own tube well today.

Same reader in all three modes; only how sources are found differs. Risk = wrong answers among answers given. Every cited number was re-found on its cited page. Code, benchmark and full traces on GitHub.

The library SerpApi built

How it works

  1. Harvest. 56 Google and Google Scholar searches over generic district templates build a library of government reports and paper abstracts. No question text is used.
  2. Read. Every table cell becomes a typed record: value, unit, statistic, place, well, date or period, spatial support, page, row.
  3. Answer or refuse. Places must exist in a table row. Comparisons are checked for the same sampling point, statistic, spatial support and time separation. Requests the sources cannot support are refused with a reason.
  4. Re-read. Before answering, each number is searched for again on the cited PDF page with a second PDF library.
  5. Fallback. If a source is missing, at most one live search, planned from cues in the question (named study → Scholar, publisher → site filter, village → quoted place).
